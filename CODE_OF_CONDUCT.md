# Code of Conduct

Pros welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, including when representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Respect privacy, credit others' work, accept constructive feedback, and make room for different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content directed at participants, personal attacks, doxxing, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [draexon](https://github.com/draexon) through the contact methods on the maintainer's profile. If no private channel is available, request one without posting names or incident details publicly. Reports will be handled discreetly; information will be shared only as needed to investigate or protect participants.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban participants for severe or repeated violations. Decisions consider the impact and context, not the contributor's status. A person affected by a decision may request a private review and provide additional context. Retaliation against anyone making a good-faith report is prohibited.
