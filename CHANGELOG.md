# Changelog

## Unreleased

- 218 React components across 15 collections, including eight consumer app blocks.
- Typed exports, ESM and CommonJS packages, light and dark themes, and reduced-motion support.
- Animated theme changes, searchable previews, custom sort menus, and keyboard shortcuts.
- Outside focus rings for grouped fields, scroll-aware navigation, and maintainer/repository links.
- MIT license, contribution guidance, community policies, and automated validation.
- A shared dropdown replaces native selects in SelectInput, WorkspaceSwitcher, TeamRoster, InviteForm, and PresenceIndicator, with keyboard navigation, type-ahead, and viewport-aware placement.
- WaterfallChart, SortableTable, TimelineChart, and TrendTicker rebuilt with real baselines, headers, axes, and consistent direction marks.
- PasswordInput, DateInput, OtpInput, SearchInput, OrbitButton, and SoundButton reworked; MentionInput now suggests people and highlights mentions.
- Layout fixes for AvatarStack, GiftCard, BudgetCard, MasonryBoard, BentoGrid, KanbanBoard, ParallaxLayers, ScrollProgress, InkHeadline, DropcapText, and dark-theme commerce surfaces.
- Gallery previews keep their source links clear of tall components, and the links stay legible in dark mode.
- The gallery is live at [pros.draexon.dev](https://pros.draexon.dev) with link previews, a sitemap, and security headers.

No package release has been published. Changes are currently available from the repository.
